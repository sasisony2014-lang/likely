Safety tips
  • Follow your local weather department and disaster authority.
  • Keep a kit ready: water, dry food, torch, power bank, medicines, documents.
  • Move away from coasts, low-lying areas and weak buildings.
  • Stay indoors during the storm; avoid flooded roads and loose wires.
  • In India, call 112 for emergencies.
